Pool Safety is among our top priorities, and everyone's responsibility. Please familiarize yourself with our 'Pool Rules' signs when you enter our pool decks and before you enter the water. Locker rooms with showers are available at the Main Pool and pool showers are available around the decks.
The Main Pool

Both the Main and Signature Pools are open daily, see hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker.

Pool Towels are found in your villa, in the master bedroom closet.

The Lazy River & Waterslide

Our 4-story Waterslide is open every day, see hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker. Riders must be 48 inches (122cm) or taller, see rules signs boards around the slide stairs. Please be sure to follow attendants' directions.

As with all of our resort amenities, wristbands are required to be worn at all times when using our pools. Pools and water facilities are subject to closure in certain weather conditions, particularly lightning and high winds. If the staff inform you that the pool is closing for lightning, please gather your belongings and exit the pool deck. Items left behind may be damaged by rain or lightning and may be turned in to lost and found.

The Lazy River is open daily, see hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker. Personal tubes are not permitted for use in the lazy river. Please use only one tube per person when visiting the lazy river to assure that there are enough tubes available for all guests who wish to use the amenity. Please see the Pool Rules below for complete information about floats in the pools.

The Signature Pool: The Quiet Pool

Located at Building Three is our Signature Pool. See hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker.

This pool is intended as a quiet "oasis" pool. All guests and all ages are welcome at The Quiet Pool, we ask only that everyone observe a few basic guidelines to assure the most pleasant experience. As with all of our facilities, anyone under the age of 12 years must be accompanied by an adult, such as a family member, guardian or babysitter. Personal conduct must be such that the safety of self and others is preserved.

As this is the quiet pool, there is no throwing balls or other items. Rough play and intentional splashing is not permitted. Electronic audio devices are permitted to be played through ear buds/earphones & kept at a low volume.

Weather Closure Protocols

⚡ Lightning

The current safety standards in place for our outdoor pools, water slide, and guest tram operations is:

  • Within 20 miles: The water slide will be closed and immediately vacated.
  • Within 15 miles: We suggest that guests begin to exit the pool decks and our resort team will start closing procedures.
  • Within 10 miles: Pool decks will be closed and all guests will be required to exit immediately. The guest tram will be suspended after 20–30 minutes of operation and conducting rounds to return guests to their buildings.

We will reopen the pool deck and resume tram operations after lightning has cleared a 10-mile radius and 30 minutes has passed since last lightning strike. The 30-minute count cannot begin until the pool deck has been cleared, so your cooperation in clearing the deck in the event of lightning is essential. Please refer to the scoreboard at the top of this page for status updates.

💨 Wind

The Water Slide must be closed if wind speeds are a constant 18 mph or gusting at 23 mph or higher. If wind speeds are 26 mph or higher, the pool deck must be closed & guest tram service must be suspended due to potential safety hazard of flying debris.

🌡 Temperature

When temperatures dip below 50°F, the slide will not be in operation. Pool technicians will make a determination on the operation of pools based on ambient water temperatures.

The Current Pool Operation Status Tracker is updated continuously by the pool staff.

Pool FAQ's

When is the pool open?

See hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker. Pools and water facilities are subject to closure in certain weather conditions, particularly lightning. If the staff inform you that the pool is closing for lightning, please gather your belongings and exit the pool deck. Items left behind may be damaged by rain or lightning and may be turned in to lost and found.

All bodies of water are subject to other closures for maintenance at any time. Please see hours posted on Current Pool Operation Status at the top of this page.

When is the waterslide open?

See hours posted on the Current Pool Operation Status Tracker. The flag at the top of the tower indicates the slide's status. If the RED flag is posted, the slide is closed. When the GREEN flag is posted, the slide is open!

What are the waterslide rules?

  • All riders must be wearing resort bands. If you're not wearing a resort band, you will be sent back to get your band.
  • Single riders only, no tandem rides. This includes small children or children with parents.
  • Riders must be no less than 42" tall.
  • Persons who are not confident swimmers should not ride.
  • Ride feet-forward, face-forward. Do not attempt to turn yourself in the slide.
  • Follow all instructions from the ride attendant.
  • The slide may close at any time for weather or any safety cause as determined by the staff.

I was given wristbands, do I really need them?

Yes! Wristbands/resort bands must be worn at all times while on resort property. Everyone in your party must be wearing a wristband/resort band while on property, to include the pool area.

Dressing up and leaving property? We're happy to replace the band when you return your cut band to the front desk! If you have lost or broken a resort band, you may go to the front desk to obtain a replacement.

I want to carry in my own food and beverages, is that okay?

Personal size coolers only may be brought into the pool deck but please anticipate Team Members asking to check the cooler, to ensure there are no glass bottles or containers inside, as they pose safety hazards on the pool deck. Food and beverages are available for purchase at the Tiki Bar at the Main Pool. Restaurant personnel also serve on the pool deck during high-volume occupancy hours. Larger coolers should be stored in your villa or vehicle, please do not leave them outside the pool gates where other people can help themselves.

Glass containers of any variety are not permitted on or around the pool decks, as broken glass poses a major safety hazard within the pool area.

Coolers found outside the gates may have a courtesy notice inserted explaining the need to return the cooler to a villa or vehicle. Coolers unmoved after 1 hour will be taken to the front desk for storage.

I like to enjoy a beverage while I'm in the pool, is that okay?

Local health code for commercial pools prohibits food or drink in the pool or on the wet deck. No items may be left near the edge of the water, or in any walkway where it might become a tripping hazard. Please keep all food and beverage items at your seat or table.

Note: it is hazardous to consume alcohol before or while using a hot tub.

Where is the smoking area?

Both of our pools are smoke-free. Smokers will need to exit the pool deck to smoke or use vaping devices.

What is the saved seat policy?

Guests may not 'save' or 'reserve' seats at the pools by leaving towels on chairs when they do not intend to be present at the pool. Any items left unsupervised for an hour or more will be condensed onto one lounger or, if all seating is occupied, personal belongings left alone will be stored in a safe location for guests to pick up at their convenience. Please see a pool team member for assistance.

What is The Signature / Quiet Pool?

The Signature Pool is 'The Quiet Pool,' and is available to all guests. Located at Building Three is our Signature Pool. This pool is intended as a quiet "oasis" pool. All guests and all ages are welcome at The Quiet Pool, we ask only that everyone observe a few basic guidelines to assure the most pleasant experience. As with all of our facilities, anyone under the age of 12 years must be accompanied by an adult, such as a family member, guardian or babysitter. Personal conduct must be such that the safety of self and others is preserved. As this is the quiet pool, there is no throwing balls or other items. Rough play and intentional splashing is not permitted. Electronic audio devices are permitted to be played through ear buds/earphones & kept at a low volume.

Can I bring a speaker to the pool?

Please don't bring any speakers to either of the pools. We have a PA system that plays music at the pool decks all day. If you bring a speaker to the pool, you will be asked to remove it.
