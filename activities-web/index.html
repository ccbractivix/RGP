Where Are The Activities Held?

The Caribe Room

The Caribe Room is where most of our Activities and Crafts will take place. It is located in the main building in the escalator lobby, on the right hand side as you exit towards the main pool.

The Mezzanine

The Mezzanine is the level located just above the Main Lobby in Building One. The easiest way to access the Mezzanine level is to ride the elevator or escalator up to the "M" floor.

The Sports Courts

The Basketball and Tennis / Pickleball Courts are on the 3rd Floor of the Southern Parking Garage at Building One by the Main Lobby. If you are looking to rent equipment, you may do so at the Front Desk, but if you are joining an activity led by our team, we'll have everything there ready to go!

If you have any difficulty finding any of these locations please ask a Team Member for assistance.

Delays and Cancellations

All rocket launches, outdoor performances, events, and activities are subject to wind and weather conditions, or availability of the host that may postpone or cancel the function. Some activities rely on the participant to be physically able to participate. In the event that Bingo cannot be held poolside, it will move into the Caribe Room. Water park and guest tram operation times are subject to change.
